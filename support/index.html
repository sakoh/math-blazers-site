Common questions

What's free, and what does the Full Game unlock?

Worlds 1 and 2, including their bonus tracks, are free. The Full Game is a one-time purchase that unlocks Worlds 3 to 8. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

How do I restore the Full Game on a new device or after reinstalling?

Open Settings in the game, tap the Full Game row, answer the grown-up question, then tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

Can my family use the Full Game too?

Yes. The Full Game supports Family Sharing, so everyone in your Family Sharing group can unlock it on their own devices with Restore Purchase.

What is the grown-up question before purchases?

It's a parental gate: a multiplication problem harder than anything the game teaches, so children can't reach purchases on their own.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your progress. Your Full Game purchase is kept by Apple and can be restored.

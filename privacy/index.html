Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Summary

Math Blazers does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet except through Apple's App Store when you make a purchase.

This policy covers the Math Blazers app for iPhone and iPad, made by Sako Hartounian ("I" or "me").

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game saves the following only on your device. It is never sent to me or anyone else.

  • Race results for each track: best place, time, accuracy, stars, streak and points
  • Unlocked worlds, Pilot Level and the hover-ship you chose
  • Whether the Full Game has been unlocked

The game never asks for a name, email address, photo, location, contacts or any other personal information. It does not use the camera or microphone.

Purchases

The one-time Full Game purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your name, Apple ID, payment details or billing address. Apple only tells the game whether the purchase is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Everything about purchasing sits behind a parental gate, so a child cannot start a purchase without an adult's help.

Children's privacy

Math Blazers is made for children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the App Store's rules for the Kids Category. Because nothing is collected, there is no data for me to share, disclose or ask parents to consent to.

Ads, tracking and third parties

Math Blazers contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party code (SDKs) and no links that leave the app. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you, and you can turn them off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its saved progress from your device. A Full Game purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored after reinstalling, using Restore Purchase.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what data it handles, I will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
